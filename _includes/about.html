A little about me

Quality engineering for software people can rely on

I’m Leslie, a software quality engineer, developer, and social justice advocate. I started as a computer engineer who loved technology and wanted to help people. That still guides my work: software should make life easier, and people should never be blamed for confusing, unusable, or defective experiences.

I bring more than five years of experience in manual and automated testing, including test planning, execution, defect reporting, documentation, and close collaboration with developers and stakeholders. I enjoy troubleshooting complex problems, asking careful questions, and turning a messy workflow into something a team can understand and trust.

I have contributed to Llave CDMX, a platform serving more than 1.6 million citizens monthly; Freshly, supporting more than one million meals each week; and Mutuo, a fintech that secured more than $100 million in financing. I currently work as a QA Analyst at The Chronicle of Higher Education.

People, community, and a life outside the screen

I lead an online economics community of more than 2,000 members. My partner and I met there while talking about economics, gender, and social justice; we fell in love and got married. We live in the United States with our dog, Timea. We work from home, play Overwatch 2, and raid EverQuest with the Machin Shin guild on Xegony.

I care about diversity and inclusion, women’s issues, inequality, public health, animal welfare, and financial literacy. My mission is to use technology, empathy, active listening, and accessible communication to help people.
